# Runway assistant knowledge

Version: 2026-10-17. Reviewed public product knowledge only. Each section has a stable source ID, title and approved citation URL. Update this file when the public guide or Mezo documentation changes. This file is the assistant's knowledge boundary, not an operations handoff. It contains no credentials or private user data. Protocol details are educational snapshots, never live quotes.

## runway | What Runway does | /docs

Runway is a Mezo testnet app for fully funded MUSD contractor payment schedules. A sender deposits all funding upfront. Entitlement accrues linearly between the confirmed start and end timestamps. The recipient claims earned MUSD with an onchain transaction. Funds do not stream automatically every second. The app combines public payment schedules, history and receipts, read-only Mezo borrowing information and owner-private workspaces with contractors and invoices. Testnet assets are for testing. Runway does not provide fiat payouts, tax certificates, payroll tax processing or guaranteed returns.

Navigation: Overview at /, New schedule at /?create=1, Workspace at /workspace, Borrowing at /borrowing, Documentation at /docs, Assistant at /assistant. A public schedule opens at /schedules/<id>. Borrowing and repayment transaction management currently opens the external Mezo app; it is not implemented as direct Runway protocol writes. Team access, contractor-submitted invoices, payroll batches and autonomous AI actions are not available.

## setup | Wallets, network and setup | /docs#getting-started

Use a compatible personal EVM browser wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby. Connect wallet lets users select their intended wallet. Unlock or install the wallet and reload if it is missing. Select Mezo testnet. Keep test BTC for transaction gas and MUSD for payment funding; their balances are different. Never share a private key or seed phrase. Runway does not ask for them. Wallet signatures authorize transactions. Signing the separate workspace login message costs no gas and does not authorize a payment. Public documentation and schedule viewing do not need workspace login. Source verification and automated tests do not constitute an independent security audit.

## funding | Fund a contractor payment | /docs#fund-a-schedule

Open New schedule. Enter the recipient, total MUSD and duration, and review them carefully. Connect the sender wallet on Mezo testnet. Funding needs enough wallet MUSD for the entire schedule and test BTC for gas. Approve the exact MUSD amount if the existing allowance is insufficient. Approval is a separate transaction and does not create or fund a schedule. Then confirm schedule creation in the wallet; creation transfers the entire MUSD funding into the payment contract. Current chain time plus a two-minute signing buffer determines the proposed start after any required approval; confirmed schedule timestamps are authoritative. Wait for confirmation and share the schedule link with the recipient. A rejected or reverted creation does not create a funded schedule; an earlier confirmed approval may remain. Check any saved pending transaction before trying again to avoid duplicates.

## claiming | Claims and earned entitlement | /docs#claim-a-payment

Open the public schedule link or find a received schedule in Overview. Check sender, recipient, total, start, end and claimable amount. Before the start there is no earned entitlement. During the schedule, entitlement grows linearly; claimable equals earned minus already claimed. At the end the full total is earned. Claiming requires an onchain transaction and gas. Anyone can trigger a claim, but funds always go to the immutable stored recipient; a different caller cannot redirect the payment. Claims can happen more than once as entitlement accrues. No claim is needed every minute. A zero claimable amount can mean the schedule has not started, earnings were already claimed, or a cancelled schedule's entitlement is fully paid.

## cancellation | Cancellation and refunds | /docs#cancellation

Only the sender can cancel, and only before the schedule ends. Cancellation freezes earned entitlement at the cancellation timestamp. Only unearned funding is refunded to the sender. Prior claims stay with the recipient, and earned but unclaimed funds remain claimable. Example: fund 100 MUSD over 100 minutes; at exactly 40 minutes, 40 MUSD is earned. If 15 MUSD was claimed before cancellation, the sender receives 60 MUSD back and the recipient may still claim 25 MUSD. Recipient total is 40 MUSD. This example excludes gas. Accrual uses chain timestamps and rounds down in the token's smallest units. Schedules cannot be edited; the contract has no admin withdrawal, upgrade or Runway payment fee. Deposited payment funds do not earn yield.

## overview | Balances and funded obligations | /docs#overview

Overview shows wallet MUSD and BTC separately and discovers sent/received schedules, loading up to 20 IDs per direction per page. Load more until discovery is complete; a schedule appearing in both directions is counted once. Loaded schedules share a source block and timestamp. Refresh obtains fresh data; failures can leave older results visible with a warning. Funded obligations cover the account's sent schedules, including earned but unpaid entitlement after cancellation. Until all pages load, totals are partial subtotals. Wallet MUSD is not money already committed to schedules, and it is not the user's borrowing debt.

## receipts | Transaction history, receipts and CSV | /docs#receipts

Open Transaction history & receipts on a schedule. Funding, claims and cancellation/refund events appear oldest first with UTC timestamps. Load more if offered; inspect the covered block range and snapshot head for completeness. Download JSON receipts for individual events or CSV of loaded history. Exports are partial while more history remains and do not update themselves after later payments. Receipts are records of onchain events, not invoices or tax certificates. Explorer links allow inspection of public transactions. The Assistant currently cannot look up a live transaction, wallet balance or receipt; use the relevant app page or explorer. Never claim a transaction succeeded without checking confirmed chain evidence.

## borrowing | Runway borrowing page and stress scenarios | /docs#borrowing

Borrowing at /borrowing reads an existing Mezo testnet position for a connected wallet or entered public address. It displays collateral, interest-inclusive debt, annual rate and loan health when source identity, price and freshness checks pass. Wallet MUSD is not loan debt. Direct borrowing, collateral adjustment and repayment transactions are not integrated in Runway; manage them in the external Mezo app. Stress scenarios hold snapshot debt and collateral fixed and change BTC price to estimate collateralization and threshold prices. They are hypothetical, do not predict prices, omit future interest and send no transaction. Stale or failed price/position reads can make health and scenarios unavailable. Refresh and check Mezo before acting. Do not recommend a personalized borrow amount or promise a safe position.

## workspace | Workspace login, contractors and invoices | /docs#workspace

Connect a personal EVM wallet on Mezo testnet, open Workspace and sign the login message. Create an owner-private workspace, save a contractor name and wallet, and create a draft invoice with an exact MUSD amount and due date. Contractor names and descriptions stay offchain. Shared team access and contractor invoice submission are not available. Create payment or Prepare payment prefills recipient, invoice amount and an opaque payment reference; review before funding. Merely opening a funding form is not a payment. Repeated funding can create duplicate payments. Sign out revokes the workspace session; disconnecting hides access but does not revoke the server session. Public schedules and payment rights do not depend on login. The Assistant cannot access these private records.

## reconciliation | Invoice payment evidence and legacy linking | /docs#workspace

Invoice business records remain draft; payment status is separate derived evidence. New invoices match confirmed schedules only when sender, recipient, exact base-unit amount and opaque reference agree. Indexing uses a 12-block confirmation buffer and verified contract/chain/token identity. The buffer is an application policy, not irreversible finality. Funded means MUSD is committed to a schedule; partially claimed means some reached the recipient; paid means the recipient claimed the full invoice amount. Cancelled shows claims and refunds separately. Remaining entitlement includes future accrual and is not necessarily claimable now. Draft means no matching confirmed schedule. Conflict means multiple schedules use the reference; explicitly select the intended schedule. Awaiting confirmation means a linked schedule is absent from the current canonical index snapshot. Unavailable means missing, behind, stale, failed or rebuilding indexing, not necessarily an unpaid invoice. Check public schedule state before funding again.

Refresh payment status checks current evidence; invoice lists also refresh every 15 seconds while active. Free backend hosting may sleep and pause indexing until wake-up/catch-up. Link existing schedule supports older payments: enter a schedule ID, and the server requires an authenticated owner plus the same sender, recipient and exact original total. A zero-reference legacy schedule can belong to one invoice only; a nonzero reference must belong to that invoice. Another schedule cannot replace an existing manual link.

## recovery | Pending transactions and troubleshooting | /docs#troubleshooting

Check the account, Mezo testnet, connection, test BTC gas balance, MUSD balance and saved pending transaction if an action is disabled. Approval alone does not create a schedule. For a pending or missing receipt, inspect the hash in the wallet and explorer: it may be pending, dropped or replaced. Runway does not automatically resubmit. The same browser can recover saved pending transactions for this chain/deployment after reload. Use Check transaction status, then Acknowledge & refresh after resolution. Removing saved tracking removes only the browser record and does not cancel a blockchain transaction. A transaction can still confirm. Saved tracking is not synced between devices. Do not submit duplicate funding before checking existing schedules and receipts.

## assistant | Assistant capabilities and privacy | /assistant

Ask Runway explains Runway and Mezo using reviewed public documentation. It can explain steps, terminology and status meanings, and link to relevant app pages and approved sources. It has no wallet connection, RPC tools, web browsing, workspace access or transaction tools. It cannot inspect a wallet, determine a live transaction outcome, sign, pay, claim, cancel, borrow or repay. Conversation questions are sent to Google's Gemini service to answer; do not paste secrets or private information. Questions and answers are held in the current page's memory, are not stored as account chat history, and clear on reload or New chat. No chat content or wallet session is shared between conversations. The server keeps bounded anonymous usage counters for abuse and spending limits. New chat clears follow-up context. Only Runway/Mezo product information is supported, not unrelated general-purpose chat, code generation, investment advice or personalized recommendations. If sources do not support an answer, explain the gap instead of guessing.

## mezo-network | Mezo network and BTC gas | https://mezo.org/docs/developers/getting-started/configure-environment/

Mezo is an EVM-compatible network. BTC is its gas token. Mezo testnet chain ID is 31611 and its HTTPS RPC is https://rpc.test.mezo.org. Mainnet and testnet are separate networks with different deployments; testnet funds and actions do not represent real mainnet funds. Runway currently targets testnet. Use official network configuration and verify chain/contract identity rather than copying an address between networks. Refer to the official developer environment page for current settings. This section is documentation, not a live RPC result.

## mezo-musd | MUSD and Bitcoin-backed borrowing | https://mezo.org/docs/users/borrow/

MUSD is Mezo's Bitcoin-backed stablecoin, designed to track the US dollar. Borrowers deposit BTC as collateral to mint MUSD rather than sell BTC. The position has debt, interest and protocol risk. Repaying the debt and applicable costs can release collateral under the protocol's rules. A peg is a design goal, not a guarantee. BTC price declines can worsen collateralization and expose positions to liquidation. MUSD, BTC and the MEZO token have different roles; a wallet's MUSD holding does not prove it has a loan. The official MUSD overview is the source for general protocol concepts. Current rates, borrowing limits and market prices are not live knowledge here.

## mezo-borrow | Borrowing and repayment through Mezo | https://mezo.org/docs/users/borrow/mint-musd/

Use the official Mezo borrowing interface, connect to the intended network, review collateral, borrowing amount and terms, and confirm transactions in the wallet. The official guide describes testnet examples separately from mainnet. Keep gas available. Loans require ongoing attention to debt and collateral; interest and fees can change what is needed to close. Do not infer debt from wallet MUSD. Exact current minimum debt, fee, rate, collateral threshold or repayment quote must be checked in the official interface and relevant chain state, not invented from this knowledge snapshot. Runway's Borrowing page provides verified reads and scenarios; the Assistant does not carry out loan management.
