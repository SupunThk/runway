# Runway assistant knowledge

Version: 2026-11-12. Reviewed public product knowledge only. Each section has a stable source ID, title and approved citation URL. Update this file when the public guide or Mezo documentation changes. This file is the assistant's knowledge boundary, not an operations handoff. It contains no credentials or private user data. Protocol details are educational snapshots, never live quotes.

## runway | What Runway does | /docs#how-it-works

Runway is a Mezo testnet app for one-time MUSD transfers and fully funded MUSD contractor payment schedules. With a schedule, a sender deposits all funding upfront. Entitlement accrues linearly between the confirmed start and end timestamps. The recipient claims earned MUSD with an onchain transaction. Funds do not stream automatically every second. The app combines public payment schedules, history and receipts, public funding proofs, Mezo borrowing reads and opt-in loan controls, client payment links, and owner-private workspaces with team roles, contractor invoice submissions, approval quorums, payroll runs and cash forecasts. Testnet assets are for testing. Runway does not provide fiat payouts, tax certificates, payroll tax processing or guaranteed returns.

Navigation: Overview at /, New payment at /?create=1, Workspace at /workspace, Borrowing at /borrowing, Documentation at /docs, Assistant at /assistant. Public routes also include schedules at /schedules/ID, client payment links at /pay/TOKEN and public funding proof at /proof/ADDRESS. Direct borrowing, collateral and repayment writes on /borrowing are production opt-in via deployment configuration; when not enabled, loan transactions open in the external Mezo app. Autonomous AI actions and custody are not available; every financial action requires explicit wallet confirmation.

## setup | Wallets, network and setup | /docs#getting-started

Use a compatible personal EVM browser wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby. Connect wallet lets users select their intended wallet. Unlock or install the wallet and reload if it is missing. Use Wallet setup beside the connected wallet to add or switch to Mezo testnet and to import the MUSD token into the wallet display. Keep test BTC for transaction gas and MUSD for payment funding; their balances are different. Never share a private key or seed phrase. Runway does not ask for them. Wallet signatures authorize transactions. Signing the separate workspace login message costs no gas and does not authorize a payment. Public documentation, schedule viewing and public funding proofs do not need workspace login. Source verification and automated tests do not constitute an independent security audit.

## manual-network-setup | Manually add Mezo testnet to your wallet | /docs#manual-network-setup

Users can add Mezo testnet directly in their wallet without connecting to Runway. Adding or switching a network does not transfer funds or spend gas. BTC is the native gas currency on Mezo; no BTC token contract import is needed for network fees. In the wallet's network settings, choose Add network manually and enter: Network name Mezo Testnet, RPC URL https://rpc.test.mezo.org, decimal Chain ID 31611, Currency symbol BTC, Native currency decimals 18, and Block explorer URL https://explorer.test.mezo.org. Save and select Mezo Testnet before sending or importing tokens.

## manual-musd-import | Manually add MUSD to your wallet | /docs#manual-musd-import

Importing MUSD makes the token visible in the wallet; it does not mint, buy or transfer tokens, and it costs no gas. A zero balance remains zero after importing. Select Mezo Testnet (chain ID 31611) in the wallet first, choose Import tokens or Custom token, and enter the Mezo testnet MUSD token contract address 0x118917a40FAF1CD7a13dB0Ef56C86De7973Ac503, symbol MUSD, and decimals 18. Do not enter Runway's payment escrow contract address when importing the MUSD token. Mainnet uses a different token address.

## funding | Fund a contractor payment | /docs#fund-a-schedule

The navigation entry is New payment. Choose Payment over time to fund a schedule and enter a whole payment period in minutes, hours, or days, up to 365 days. Choose One-time payment to transfer the entire MUSD amount directly to the recipient, with no approval, duration, or claim step. Direct payments cannot be cancelled and have explorer receipts rather than schedule entries.

Open New payment and choose Payment over time. Enter the recipient, total MUSD and duration, and review them carefully. Connect the sender wallet on Mezo testnet. Funding needs enough wallet MUSD for the entire schedule and test BTC for gas. Approve the exact MUSD amount if the existing allowance is insufficient. Approval is a separate transaction and does not create or fund a schedule. Then confirm schedule creation in the wallet; creation transfers the entire MUSD funding into the payment contract. Current chain time plus a two-minute signing buffer determines the proposed start after any required approval; confirmed schedule timestamps are authoritative. Wait for confirmation and share the schedule link with the recipient. A rejected or reverted creation does not create a funded schedule; an earlier confirmed approval may remain. Check any saved pending transaction before trying again to avoid duplicates.

## one-time-payment | Send a one-time payment | /docs#one-time-payment

Open New payment and choose One-time payment, then enter the recipient wallet and exact MUSD amount. No duration, token allowance approval or recipient claim transaction is required. Confirm Send MUSD in the wallet with enough MUSD for the payment and test BTC for gas. The full amount transfers immediately to the recipient, cannot be cancelled, and does not create a schedule entry. For a one-time invoice prepared from Workspace, a matching transfer is linked automatically and becomes paid after the 12-block confirmation buffer. If automatic linking is interrupted, copy the transaction hash and use Link payment transaction beside the invoice instead of paying a second time. Each transfer can settle at most one invoice or payment request.

## claiming | Claims and earned entitlement | /docs#claim-a-payment

Open the public schedule link or find a received schedule in Overview. Check sender, recipient, total, start, end and claimable amount. Before the start there is no earned entitlement. During the schedule, entitlement grows linearly; claimable equals earned minus already claimed. At the end the full total is earned. Claiming requires an onchain transaction and gas. Anyone can trigger a claim, but funds always go to the immutable stored recipient; a different caller cannot redirect the payment. Claims can happen more than once as entitlement accrues. No claim is needed every minute. A zero claimable amount can mean the schedule has not started, earnings were already claimed, or a cancelled schedule's entitlement is fully paid.

## cancellation | Cancellation and refunds | /docs#cancellation

Only the sender can cancel, and only before the schedule ends. Cancellation freezes earned entitlement at the cancellation timestamp. Only unearned funding is refunded to the sender. Prior claims stay with the recipient, and earned but unclaimed funds remain claimable. Example: fund 100 MUSD over 100 minutes; at exactly 40 minutes, 40 MUSD is earned. If 15 MUSD was claimed before cancellation, the sender receives 60 MUSD back and the recipient may still claim 25 MUSD. Recipient total is 40 MUSD. This example excludes gas. Accrual uses chain timestamps and rounds down in the token's smallest units. Schedules cannot be edited; the contract has no admin withdrawal, upgrade or Runway payment fee. Deposited payment funds do not earn yield.

## overview | Balances and funded obligations | /docs#overview

Overview shows wallet MUSD, test BTC for gas, and an optional validated MEZO token balance separately, and discovers sent/received schedules up to 20 IDs per direction per page. Load more until discovery is complete; a schedule appearing in both directions is counted once. Loaded schedules share a source block and timestamp. Refresh obtains fresh data; failures can leave older results visible with a warning. Funded obligations cover the account's sent schedules, including earned but unpaid entitlement after cancellation. Until all pages load, totals are partial subtotals. Wallet MUSD is not money already committed to schedules, and it is not the user's borrowing debt.

## runway-planner | Runway planner | /docs#runway-planner

On Borrowing, the Runway planner combines the connected wallet's current sent schedules, available wallet MUSD and verified loan snapshot. Payroll coverage assumes each active stream renews immediately at its existing rate when its escrow ends, while keeping any user-specified repayment reserve out of renewal spending. Existing escrow cannot be reassigned between recipients, future-starting schedules are excluded and disclosed, and rates round up conservatively. Users can model 1–24 planning months (30 days each), a BTC price drop and a target collateral ratio to compare payment rates against protocol-aware borrowing capacity (accounting for issuance fees, exemptions, minimum debt, opening reserve, Recovery Mode and stored trove limits). The planner never borrows or submits transactions automatically.

## funding-plans | Saved borrowing and payment plans | /docs#funding-plans

On New payment, choosing Save payment plan & review funding options saves the intended recipient, exact MUSD amount and payment type or duration in browser storage for the connected wallet and chain. If wallet MUSD is short, the founder can review and execute a Mezo borrowing action first, then return to fund the exact saved payment terms without re-entering them or borrowing twice. Borrowing and payment funding remain separate wallet-confirmed transactions; a rejected funding step does not undo a confirmed loan. Reloading checks saved transaction hashes and blocks duplicate submissions until pending hashes resolve after the 12-block confirmation buffer.

## client-payment-links | Client payment links | /docs#client-payment-links

In a signed-in Workspace, owners and managers can create a client payment request with a public business name, memo, recipient address, exact MUSD amount and expiry, then share its public /pay/TOKEN link. Private client labels and workspace notes remain private. A payer opens /pay/TOKEN without signing in, connects a wallet on Mezo testnet, and sends a direct MUSD transfer after network, balance, gas reserve and simulation checks. The request is marked paid once a canonical matching transfer reaches the 12-block confirmation buffer. One transfer can settle at most one request or invoice across the workspace, and an expired link blocks new transfers while still allowing recovery of a qualifying transfer mined before expiry.

## public-funding-proof | Public funding proof | /docs#public-funding-proof

Anyone can open /proof/ADDRESS for a sender wallet address without connecting a wallet or signing in to inspect a sender's funded schedule commitments and contract-wide MUSD escrow balance at a single pinned confirmed block (using the 12-block buffer). Committed MUSD equals remaining recipient entitlement (original funding minus refunds and claims, including earned unclaimed amounts on cancelled schedules). Unloaded pages or failed schedule reads are explicitly labelled as partial subtotals. The proof verifies displayed escrow commitments at the source block; it does not establish overall founder solvency, future funding, loan safety or business revenue.

## receipts | Transaction history, receipts and CSV | /docs#receipts

Open Transaction history & receipts on a schedule. Funding, claims and cancellation/refund events appear oldest first with UTC timestamps. Load more if offered; inspect the covered block range and snapshot head for completeness. Download JSON receipts for individual events or CSV of loaded history. Exports are partial while more history remains and do not update themselves after later payments. Receipts are records of onchain events, not invoices or tax certificates. Explorer links allow inspection of public transactions. The Assistant currently cannot look up a live transaction, wallet balance or receipt; use the relevant app page or explorer. Never claim a transaction succeeded without checking confirmed chain evidence.

## borrowing | Runway borrowing page and stress scenarios | /docs#borrowing

Borrowing at /borrowing reads an existing Mezo testnet position for a connected wallet or entered public address. It displays collateral, interest-inclusive debt, annual rate and loan health when source identity, price and freshness checks pass. Wallet MUSD is not loan debt. When borrowing writes are enabled on the deployment, the connected wallet can review, simulate and sign trove opening, additional MUSD borrowing, BTC collateral addition and partial repayment directly on /borrowing, with saved transaction recovery and Borrow-to-Fund plan tracking; when disabled, loan management opens in the external Mezo app. Stress scenarios hold snapshot debt and collateral fixed and change BTC price to estimate collateralization and threshold prices. They are hypothetical, do not predict prices, omit future interest and send no transaction. Stale or failed price/position reads can make health and scenarios unavailable. Refresh and check Mezo before acting. Do not recommend a personalized borrow amount or promise a safe position.

## workspace | Workspace login, contractors and invoices | /docs#workspace

Invoice creation supports a payment type: One-time payment or Payment over time. Prepare payment preserves that type. For one-time invoices, successful direct MUSD transfers are linked automatically. If linking is interrupted, use Link payment transaction with the transaction hash from the wallet or saved transaction; do not pay again. The backend verifies the owner as sender, the MUSD token, exact recipient and amount, a successful Transfer event, a canonical block after invoice creation, and a 12-block confirmation buffer before showing paid. Each transfer can belong to only one invoice or payment request. Status remains awaiting confirmation until the buffer is met; unavailable or conflicting evidence is never treated as paid. Refresh rechecks chain evidence, including reorgs. Direct invoice tracking works independently of the schedule indexer.

Connect a personal EVM wallet on Mezo testnet, open Workspace and sign the login message, or use the ERC-1271 contract-wallet login flow with a verified onchain signature. Create an owner-private workspace, save a contractor name and wallet, and create a draft invoice with an exact MUSD amount and due date. Contractor names and descriptions stay offchain. Owners can invite specific wallets as manager, approver, viewer or contractor; access begins after the invited wallet accepts. Registered contractors can submit their own invoices without viewing other workspace records. Create payment or Prepare payment prefills recipient, invoice amount and an opaque payment reference; review before funding. Merely opening a funding form is not a payment. Repeated funding can create duplicate payments. Sign out revokes the workspace session; disconnecting hides access but does not revoke the server session. Public schedules and payment rights do not depend on login. The Assistant cannot access these private records.

## reconciliation | Invoice payment evidence and legacy linking | /docs#workspace

Invoice business records remain draft; payment status is separate derived evidence. Scheduled invoices match confirmed schedules only when sender, recipient, exact base-unit amount and opaque reference agree. Indexing uses a 12-block confirmation buffer and verified contract/chain/token identity. The buffer is an application policy, not irreversible finality. Funded means MUSD is committed to a schedule; partially claimed means some reached the recipient; paid means the recipient claimed the full invoice amount. Cancelled shows claims and refunds separately. Remaining entitlement includes future accrual and is not necessarily claimable now. Draft means no matching confirmed schedule. Conflict means multiple schedules use the reference; explicitly select the intended schedule. Awaiting confirmation means a linked schedule is absent from the current canonical index snapshot. Unavailable means missing, behind, stale, failed or rebuilding indexing, not necessarily an unpaid invoice. Check public schedule state before funding again.

Refresh payment status checks current evidence; invoice lists also refresh every 15 seconds while active. Free backend hosting may sleep and pause indexing until wake-up/catch-up. Link existing schedule supports older payments: enter a schedule ID, and the server requires an authenticated owner plus the same sender, recipient and exact original total. A zero-reference legacy schedule can belong to one invoice only; a nonzero reference must belong to that invoice. Another schedule cannot replace an existing manual link.

## recovery | Pending transactions and troubleshooting | /docs#troubleshooting

Check the account, Mezo testnet, connection, test BTC gas balance, MUSD balance and saved pending transaction if an action is disabled. Approval alone does not create a schedule. For a pending or missing receipt, inspect the hash in the wallet and explorer: it may be pending, dropped or replaced. Runway does not automatically resubmit. The same browser can recover saved pending transactions for this chain/deployment after reload. Use Check transaction status, then Acknowledge & refresh after resolution. Removing saved tracking removes only the browser record and does not cancel a blockchain transaction. A transaction can still confirm. Saved tracking is not synced between devices. Do not submit duplicate funding before checking existing schedules and receipts.

## assistant | Assistant capabilities and privacy | /docs#assistant

Ask Runway explains Runway and Mezo using reviewed public documentation. It can explain steps, terminology and status meanings, and link to relevant app pages and approved sources. It has no wallet connection, RPC tools, web browsing, workspace access or transaction tools. It cannot inspect a wallet, determine a live transaction outcome, sign, pay, claim, cancel, borrow or repay. Conversation questions are sent to Google's Gemini service to answer; do not paste secrets or private information. Questions and answers are held in the current page's memory, are not stored as account chat history, and clear on reload or New chat. No chat content or wallet session is shared between conversations. The server keeps bounded anonymous usage counters for abuse and spending limits. New chat clears follow-up context. Only Runway/Mezo product information is supported, not unrelated general-purpose chat, code generation, investment advice or personalized recommendations. If sources do not support an answer, explain the gap instead of guessing.

## trust-and-limits | What to know about this release | /docs#trust-and-limits

Runway is a Mezo testnet prototype. Onchain payment addresses, amounts, timing and events are public; sharing a schedule link or public proof link lets anyone inspect those onchain details. The RunwayPayments contract has no schedule editing, admin withdrawals, upgrade proxy or Runway protocol fee, and deposited funds do not earn yield. The MUSD token contract on Mezo testnet has owner-managed mint, burn and system-contract privileges, so escrow solvency depends on token governance as well as the payment contract. Source verification on the Mezo testnet explorer and automated unit, browser and Foundry invariant tests do not replace an independent security audit.

## mezo-network | Mezo network and BTC gas | https://mezo.org/docs/developers/getting-started/configure-environment/

Mezo is an EVM-compatible network. BTC is its gas token. Mezo testnet chain ID is 31611 and its HTTPS RPC is https://rpc.test.mezo.org. Mainnet and testnet are separate networks with different deployments; testnet funds and actions do not represent real mainnet funds. Runway currently targets testnet. Use official network configuration and verify chain/contract identity rather than copying an address between networks. Refer to the official developer environment page for current settings. This section is documentation, not a live RPC result.

## mezo-musd | MUSD and Bitcoin-backed borrowing | https://mezo.org/docs/users/borrow/

MUSD is Mezo's Bitcoin-backed stablecoin, designed to track the US dollar. Borrowers deposit BTC as collateral to mint MUSD rather than sell BTC. The position has debt, interest and protocol risk. Repaying the debt and applicable costs can release collateral under the protocol's rules. A peg is a design goal, not a guarantee. BTC price declines can worsen collateralization and expose positions to liquidation. MUSD, BTC and the MEZO token have different roles; a wallet's MUSD holding does not prove it has a loan. The official MUSD overview is the source for general protocol concepts. Current rates, borrowing limits and market prices are not live knowledge here.

## mezo-borrow | Borrowing and repayment through Mezo | https://mezo.org/docs/users/borrow/mint-musd/

Borrowers can manage positions through the official Mezo borrowing interface or, when enabled on the Runway deployment, through Runway's reviewed borrowing controls on /borrowing. Always connect to the intended network, review collateral, borrowing amount, issuance fees, minimum debt and collateral ratio terms, and confirm transactions in the wallet. The official guide describes testnet examples separately from mainnet. Keep BTC gas available. Loans require ongoing attention to debt and collateral; interest and fees can change what is needed to close. Do not infer debt from wallet MUSD. Exact current minimum debt, fee, rate, collateral threshold or repayment quote must be checked in the live interface and relevant chain state, not invented from this knowledge snapshot. The Assistant does not carry out loan transactions.

## team-operations | Team approvals and cash operations | /docs#team-operations

Workspaces support accepted wallet-specific invitations, manager/approver/viewer/contractor roles, private contractor invoice submissions, immutable payment terms and versioned approval quorums. Revoked approvers no longer count, policy changes invalidate prior approvals, and a submitter cannot approve their own submission. Only the workspace owner prepares and signs payments. A payroll run groups 1–50 approved invoices with an exact MUSD total and confirmation-derived progress; each payment is reviewed and signed individually in the wallet, never executed as an automatic batch transfer. Current approvals are rechecked before wallet prompts and payment linking. Offchain workspace policies do not restrict transactions outside Runway.

Weekly/monthly recurring plans prepare one reviewed due date at a time, with duplicate/crash recovery and month-end handling. Missed dates remain visible; pausing stops new preparation, not existing onchain payments. Dated cash forecasts use an explicitly entered wallet balance, one repayment reserve and optional session-local expected-receipt assumptions; they exclude already funded escrow and receipts already in wallet cash. Unknown payment evidence and record/projection limits are disclosed as partial. Reminders are opt-in per wallet/workspace and refresh only while the page is open, with no email, push delivery or background transaction agent. Scoped CSV reports neutralize spreadsheet formulas, and private one-year audit records log actor, action and outcome.

ERC-1271 contract-wallet login validates a fully approved signature on Mezo testnet and rechecks it on each authenticated request; RPC errors deny access, and actual multisig transaction compatibility still requires installed-wallet validation. The Assistant cannot access private workspace or team records or sign/submit transactions. The separate RunwayMilestones contract is prepared and tested offline, but is not deployed or integrated into app pages until a user-signed deployment gate is completed.
