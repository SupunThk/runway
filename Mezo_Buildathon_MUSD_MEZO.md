## Overview

The **Mezo Buildathon** is a two-Wave builder program hosted on AKINDO for teams extending the utility of **MUSD** and **MEZO** — Mezo's Bitcoin-backed stablecoin and native token. Built by Thesis (the team behind tBTC, Fold, and Taho).

**Core requirement:** Your project **must integrate MUSD and/or MEZO**.  
You can deploy on **Mezo, Ethereum, Base, or BNB Chain**.

This is **not** a one-shot hackathon. Teams build across two connected Waves, ship working products, get evaluated by the Mezo core team, and carry momentum forward:
- **Wave 1** rewards early shipping.
- **Wave 2** carries the larger pool and rewards depth.

**Total Grant Pool:** **$7,000 MEZO** (distributed on Ethereum)  
- Wave 1: $2,000 MEZO  
- Wave 2: $5,000 MEZO  

Rewards are distributed **proportionally to evaluation points** (not fixed top-N winners).

## What is Mezo?

Mezo is the Bitcoin finance app that lets people **live off their Bitcoin without selling it**. Borrow, earn, and spend — permissionlessly and without banks.

**Key assets:**
- **MUSD**: Mezo's native stablecoin, 100% backed by Bitcoin reserves, designed to hold 1:1 with the US dollar.
- **MEZO**: Mezo's native token.

Mezo is **EVM-compatible** (Solidity works). The stack includes Bitcoin-collateralized borrowing, yield vaults, and the full Mezo app surface.

## Tracks

Two tracks. You can submit to **one or both**.

### Track 1: DeFi (Borrowing, Lending, Looping, and Yield)

**Challenge:** *How might we expand MUSD and MEZO utility as collateral and productive capital across DeFi?*

Build protocols and strategies that make MUSD and MEZO a core building block in DeFi: composable collateral and yield-bearing capital.

**Example projects:**
- Lending protocols accepting MUSD/MEZO as collateral (single-chain or cross-chain)
- Looping strategies for MUSD/MEZO positions (leveraged yield farming)
- Yield-generating vaults and auto-compounding strategies for MUSD/MEZO
- MUSD/MEZO liquidity pools and DEX integrations on other chains
- Staking derivatives for MUSD/MEZO

**Requirement:** MUSD/MEZO integration.

### Track 2: Access and Distribution for MUSD/MEZO

**Challenge:** *How might we make MUSD and MEZO easier to discover, access, and use?*

Build the tools, bots, and interfaces that reduce friction for users interacting with Mezo's ecosystem — meeting users where they already are.

**Example projects:**
- Telegram or WhatsApp bots for MUSD/MEZO transactions and monitoring
- Matching and discovery platforms
- Marketplaces and aggregators for Mezo ecosystem products
- Cross-platform wallets and interfaces that simplify MUSD/MEZO access

**Requirement:** MUSD or MEZO integration.

### What Mezo Is Looking For (Scoring Principles)

- **Supported chains:** Mezo, Ethereum, Base, or BNB Chain (Mezo testnet welcome)
- **Integration is mandatory** — no integration = no eligibility
- **Extend, don't detour** — projects that extend existing Mezo products are prioritized
- **Composability wins** — plugging into existing DeFi infrastructure scores higher than isolated apps
- **Long-term commitment counts** — existing products + integration, or new projects with credible post-hackathon plans

## Rewards & Distribution

- **Total pool:** $7,000 paid in **MEZO tokens** (on Ethereum)
- After each Wave, Mezo core team allocates points across eligible submissions
- Each project's share of the pool = its share of total points
- Example: 20% of points → 20% of that Wave's pool
- No fixed number of winners — strong projects earn more
- Grant recipients must complete **Mezo's KYB** to receive rewards

## Judging Criteria

**Two-stage process:**

1. **Stage 1 – Screening (AKINDO DevRel)**  
   Checks: MUSD/MEZO integration present, working deployment on supported chain, all submission materials provided. Non-qualifying submissions are not forwarded.

2. **Stage 2 – Evaluation (Mezo Core Team)**  
   Points allocated based on:

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Mezo Integration** | 30% | Effective and meaningful use of MUSD or MEZO. Depth of integration matters more than surface-level usage |
| **Product & Business Viability** | 30% | How directly the project extends MUSD/MEZO utility in line with the track's challenge. Problem-solution fit, business viability, complementarity with Mezo ecosystem |
| **Technical Implementation** | 20% | Code quality, architecture, security, and whether the solution is genuinely composable and deployable (not just a demo) |
| **User Experience** | 10% | Design, usability, and accessibility |
| **Submission Materials Quality** | 10% | Demo clarity and pitch effectiveness |
